# FAQs

## What age range are your products suitable for?

We recommend that plushies and keyrings are for ages 14 up. None of our products are toys, and whilst we try to ensure high safety standards, they are not suitable for children.

## How do I wash an item?

Items should be only sponge/spot cleaned. Wallets and bookmarks in particular will lose rigidity if fully immersed in water

## Are your items vegan?

Yes! All fur used is faux fur

## Can I buy online?

Not yet, hopefully soon! Meanwhile please check our social media for the next market

## I can't see the flag I want?

We try to cover a range of identities, if you can't see the one you're after please let us know, we do expand the range regulary

## Can I make my own version?

Most of the designs are created by us and not for sale, but you can find the original large bat plushie pattern [on BeezeeArt.com](https://www.beezeeart.com/products/stuffed-animal-bat-sewing-pattern-plush-toy-pattern-pdf)
